# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `alifbo` community welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss evidence and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, especially in linguistic questions.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and moderation gracefully.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/azakapro). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
